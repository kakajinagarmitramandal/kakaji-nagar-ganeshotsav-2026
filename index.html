01 / ABOUT US

More Than A Celebration

A journey of faith, tradition and community.

ESTABLISHED 31 AUGUST 1992

Kakaji Nagar Sarvajanik Shree Ganesh Utsav Mitra Mandal

Kakaji Nagar Sarvajanik Shree Ganesh Utsav Mitra Mandal was established on 31 August 1992 in Goregaon West, Mumbai.

For more than three decades, our Mandal has brought the community together through Ganeshotsav, devotion, culture and togetherness.

In 2026, we are celebrating our 35th year and inviting businesses and organizations to become part of our celebration through sponsorship and advertising.

Every year, our Ganpati celebration brings together faith, creativity, culture and community spirit.
